MoneyMate Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 15, 2026

MoneyMate is a personal finance management application developed by Tavrix Labs. This Privacy Policy explains how MoneyMate handles information when you use the app.

1. Information You Enter

MoneyMate may allow you to enter information such as income, expenses, budgets, savings goals, EMI details, debt details, transaction notes, and related financial records.

2. How Your Data Is Stored

MoneyMate is designed primarily for offline use. Finance records are stored locally on your device using the app's local storage/database mechanisms. The app does not require an account or sign-in for its core features.

3. Backup and Restore

If you use Backup or Restore, the app may create or import a backup file containing information stored in MoneyMate. You are responsible for keeping exported backup files secure and for choosing where they are stored or shared.

4. Data Sharing

MoneyMate does not sell your personal information. Its core offline functionality does not require your finance records to be sent to Tavrix Labs. If you voluntarily share a backup file or other app-generated information with another person or service, that sharing is under your control.

5. Financial Services

MoneyMate provides personal finance tracking and calculation tools only. It does not provide banking, loans, money transfers, investment services, cryptocurrency services, insurance services, or financial advice.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Your finance records are primarily stored on your device. You can remove records using available app controls or by uninstalling the app, subject to your device's operating-system behavior and any backups you have created. Backup files stored outside the app must be deleted separately.

7. Security

We take reasonable steps to design MoneyMate to keep locally stored information within the app's storage environment. However, no device, software, or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure. Please protect your device and backup files appropriately.

8. Children's Privacy

MoneyMate is a general personal finance utility and is not specifically directed to children. We do not knowingly request personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app or its data practices change. The latest version will be made available at the public Privacy Policy URL used for MoneyMate.
